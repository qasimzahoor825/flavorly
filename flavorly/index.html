Creamy Garlic Chicken

MA
Maya AnwarPublished 3 days ago

Juicy boneless chicken breasts are pan-seared until golden, then finished in a rich garlic parmesan cream sauce with baby spinach and a squeeze of lemon. A weeknight dinner that tastes like a slow Sunday.

4.9
212 ratings · 38,204 saves

Tap a star to leave your rating

Ingredients

Scaling servings:4portions
Chicken breasts4 fillets
Heavy cream300 ml
Garlic cloves5, minced
Parmesan60 g, grated
Baby spinach120 g
Butter & olive oil1 tbsp each
Lemon juice½ tbsp
Salt, pepper & thymeto taste

Instructions 6 numbered steps

1

Season & sear. Pat the chicken dry, season both sides, and sear in a hot pan with butter and olive oil until golden — about 5 minutes per side. Rest on a plate.

2

Bloom the garlic. Lower the heat, add the minced garlic and thyme, and cook 30 seconds until fragrant without browning.

3

Build the sauce. Pour in the cream, add the parmesan, and stir continuously until smooth and gently bubbling.

4

Return the chicken. Nestle the chicken back into the sauce with any resting juices, spooning sauce over the tops.

5

Wilt the spinach. Fold in the spinach and cook until just wilted, 2–3 minutes. Correct seasoning with salt and pepper.

6

Finish & serve. Finish with lemon juice, a little extra parmesan, and serve with crusty bread or rice.
